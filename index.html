Seams

Baseball motion capture and coaching, from the camera you already own.

Seams films one repetition, reads your body frame by frame, and turns it into measurements with target ranges — then a coach reads those numbers and tells you what to fix. Four disciplines: hitting, pitching, fielding and baserunning.

Common questions

Does my video leave my phone?

The video file never does. It is recorded, analysed and stored on the device, and it is deleted when you delete the session. To write your coaching, a handful of still frames — with the detected skeleton drawn over them — are sent to the AI provider along with your measurements. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what is sent and to whom.

Do I need a subscription?

Yes. Seams requires an active subscription to use. There is no free tier and no trial period; billing starts when you confirm the purchase. You can cancel at any time in your Apple ID settings.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Seams → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the subscription stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. We cannot cancel or refund an App Store subscription on your behalf — refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but the app still asks me to subscribe.

Tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen, and make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, and you do not need to ask us. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. To also erase the copy held in our backend, email us from the address you want us to look for and we will remove it. See Privacy.

Is this medical or physiotherapy advice?

No. Seams is a training tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any injury or condition. Read the Terms of Use before you act on anything it tells you.
