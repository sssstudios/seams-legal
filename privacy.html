Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Effective 1 November 2026

This explains what SSS STUDIOS LLC does with your information in the Seams app. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

Your video never leaves your phone. It is recorded, analysed and stored on the device, and deleting the session deletes the file.

A few still frames do leave, and only when you ask for written coaching: up to eight single images with the detected skeleton drawn over them, sent together with your measurements so the coach can read what the numbers cannot.

We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising in Seams.

What stays on your phone

  • The video of every session.
  • The full pose track — the joint positions for every analysed frame.
  • Your sessions, measurements, plan and history, in a local database.

The app works without a network. Nothing in this list is required to leave the device for the measurements to be produced.

What we collect, and why

the name you choose, age, handedness, level, position, and the goal you type		 To set the target ranges and let the coach address you. The name is whatever you type — it does not have to be your real one.

Subscription status To know whether your subscription is active. Handled by RevenueCat; we never receive your payment details.

There is no login

Seams does not ask for an email address or a password. Your backup is tied to an anonymous identifier created on your device the first time it syncs. We cannot tell who you are from it, and we cannot connect two devices to the same person unless you tell us.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase — hosts the database and the function that writes the coaching. It holds the measurements and profile listed above.
  • Anthropic — receives the measurements, the profile fields and the key frames at the moment of analysis, and returns the written coaching. It is a processor acting on our instructions; under its commercial terms this content is not used to train its models.
  • RevenueCat and Apple — handle the subscription. Apple bills you; we only learn whether a subscription is active.

That is the whole list. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

Measurements stay until you delete them. Key frames are not retained by us; the AI provider holds them only for as long as its own operational and abuse-monitoring policies require. Delete the app and everything on the device goes with it.

Your choices

  • Do not sync at all. Seams measures, scores and keeps history with the network off. Backup and written coaching are the only features that need it.
  • Delete a session and its video and measurements go with it.
  • Delete everything we hold. Email Support@ssssstudios.com and we will erase the backup. Because there is no login, say which device so we can find the right record.
  • Ask for a copy. Same address, same reason.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights over your personal data — to access, correct, delete, or object to processing. Write to us and we will honour them.

Children

Seams is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are a parent and believe a child under 13 has used the app, email us and we will delete what we hold. Between 13 and 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms of Use first.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. The backup database restricts every row to the anonymous identifier that wrote it. No system is perfectly secure, so keep in mind that the safest copy of your video is the one that never left your phone — which is where it stays.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes with it. A change that materially affects what we collect will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
